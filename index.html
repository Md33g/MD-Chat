About MD Chat

A small, text-and-image room chat demo.

Disclaimer

MD Chat is a demo project. Messages are transmitted through a public WebSocket relay and are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not share sensitive information.

Privacy

No personal data is stored on a server. Username, avatar, and settings are saved only in your browser’s localStorage. Room history is cached in IndexedDB on your device.

Public rooms

Room names are not passwords. Anyone who knows or guesses the room name can join. Do not use MD Chat for private or sensitive conversations.

No accounts, no tracking. Built with Yjs and the public wss://demos.yjs.dev/ws relay. Images are compressed and sent inside the shared document; the relay may reset at any time.

About MD Chat

A small, text-only room chat demo.

Disclaimer

MD Chat is a demo project. Messages are transmitted through a public WebSocket relay and are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not share sensitive information.

Privacy

No personal data is stored on a server. Username and avatar are saved only in your browser’s localStorage.

Public rooms

Room names are not passwords. Anyone who knows or guesses the room name can join, see the messages, and appear in the room. Do not use MD Chat for private or sensitive conversations.

No accounts, no tracking, no media uploads. Built with Yjs and the public wss://demos.yjs.dev/ws relay. Rooms are ephemeral and may reset when the relay restarts.
